## Analytics

YouTube defines stayed-to-watch around the initial seconds of a Short. Engaged views exclude loops; Shorts average view duration and average percentage viewed are calculated among viewers who stayed. The current help page also carries a **24 August 2026** view-counting update across formats. Preserve metric definitions and capture dates when comparing historical examples. [YouTube content-performance definitions](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12220281?hl=en).

**Researcher application:** inspect opening decisions and later viewing behavior separately. A percentage without its definition, denominator, time window and video length cannot support a precise creative verdict. Unknown corpus publication dates prevent confidently assigning older examples to a counting regime.

## Editing A Published Upload

YouTube's editor supports trimming the beginning, middle or end while retaining the upload URL, count and comments. The page describes restrictions and states that saved editor changes cannot be reverted using the former restoration feature. [YouTube trimming guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9057455?hl=en).

**Researcher application:** preserve the original asset and pre-edit metrics; inspect the actual scene before recommending an edit. Treat the edited version as a different creative version. This study does not establish that trimming causes distribution to restart or that it repairs initial swiping.

## Reuse And Monetization

YouTube evaluates reused content and generic or repetitive production at channel level. Its reused-content monetization assessment is separate from copyright enforcement and permission; examples that meet one assessment may still be subject to other policies. The current policy also discusses originality and substantive contribution when automated tools or templates are used. [YouTube channel monetization policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en).

**Researcher application:** copying a successful format, holding permission, adding surface edits and being eligible for monetization are different questions. A caption's sales claim cannot establish a particular video's rights or eligibility. Inspect the proposed content and applicable current rules before treating a tactic as deployable.

## Still Open

- No independent confirmation of a universal trust score, account warm-up recipe, fixed view ceiling or guaranteed recovery tactic has been established in this study.
- Source earnings and testimonials lack a verified denominator, representative outcomes and consistent cost accounting.
- The audience-retention help page could not be refreshed during this check. No new timing claim is inferred from that failed fetch.
- The user's actual channel data, source asset and retention chart remain necessary for an individualized verdict.
