## Summary

The creator describes “viewbombing” as an alleged abrupt reach collapse caused by YouTube AI misclassifying channel views as botting, especially on faceless or low-trust channels. He attributes some risk to deleting a struck or claimed video instead of appealing, and recommends keeping such videos private, publishing face-led long-form videos, disabling “publish to subscriptions feed,” and continuing uploads. He describes an 8 to 12 day trust-score “test and rebalance” period. Later, he recommends contacting YouTube through Twitter or support about suspected errors and says temporary live streams may boost reach. He reports running 36 channels, some terminations, and limited recovery after trying the suggested changes. These mechanisms and outcomes are his claims and personal experience; he says causes are not specifically identified, the issue is ongoing, and available solutions may have limited effect.
