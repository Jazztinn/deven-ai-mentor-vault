## Make visual stories legible across language groups

Card: storytelling-B003-K001 · Kind: source advice.

Speakers recommend making visible action carry enough of the story that viewers can follow without captions or shared spoken language. Examples include a gift reveal, game action clarified with effects/reactions, a wordless comedy clip, and speech-heavy clipping that may still entertain through editing. Some permit text for names. **Conditions:** Most applicable when story action is visually clear and target audience spans language groups; spoken-language-dependent clips may need context or captions.
