## Summary

Creator’s overall model says Shorts performance should be read through swipe ratio and average view duration together, with swipe ratio reflecting whether viewers stay beyond three seconds. He describes early testing across audience or metadata groups, followed by more distribution to topic groups with stronger swipe ratios, and gives hypothetical checkpoints around three, six, and 24 hours. He recommends patience, improving swipe ratio when it is around 70–75% during a view plateau, and checking average view duration or niche size when swipe ratio is already high. For longer-term assessment, he recommends reviewing a 30-day graph for higher highs and lows and checking new, casual, and regular viewer shares if swipe ratio is high but casual viewership is low. He proposes casual viewers above 50% during growth. Creator’s platform mechanics, thresholds, projections, view results, revenue claims, and guaranteed-plan language remain attributed and unverified; his swipe-ratio-plus-retention formula is garbled and not reproducible.
