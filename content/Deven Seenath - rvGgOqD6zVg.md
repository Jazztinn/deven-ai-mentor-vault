## Summary

Video argues that product research should focus on marketing angles and audiences, especially whether content gives people a reason to share it. ## Actions From This Record

- **Source instruction:** For each candidate product, list distinct audiences and uses, then test an angle for one audience. (Findings 1, 2, 3, 4, 6.)
- **Source instruction:** Adapt product videos to platform; for TikTok, use faster pacing and get to the point quickly. (Findings 11, 12.)
- **Researcher inference:** Treat organic audience-building as an experiment, measuring results before relying on claims about views, costs, or margins. (Findings 13, 14, 15.)
- **Source instruction:** When evaluating a product angle, ask whether people would share the resulting content with someone else. (Findings 17, 18, 22, 28.)
- **Researcher inference:** Track shares and average view duration when testing Instagram videos; treat the speaker’s suggested ranges as hypotheses to test for the account. (Findings 20, 21, 30.)
- **Source instruction:** Build product research skill through repeated practice, such as making many videos or testing many products. (Findings 29.)
