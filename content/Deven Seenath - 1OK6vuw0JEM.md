## Summary

Promotional case study featuring two partners who already had an animation brand and about 100,000 followers before joining a coaching program. They credit Shorts with helping expand reach across platforms, increase long-form activity, and promote merchandise, while acknowledging that attribution is difficult. After a YouTube demonetization event, they say their earlier dependence on YouTube pushed them to diversify, including monetizing Spotify and X and hiring editors. They report large audience, revenue, merchandise, and RPM figures, but those figures and the claimed causal links are unverified testimonials with unclear or approximate periods. The host promotes the program and claims access to a YouTube representative; even the host says representatives are not guaranteed to fix demonetization. Their accounts suggest testing cross-platform distribution and revenue diversification, but do not establish typical results, platform policy, or guaranteed earnings.
