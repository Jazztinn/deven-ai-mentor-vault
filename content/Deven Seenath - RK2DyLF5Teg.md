## Summary

Deven Seenath argues that people absorb habits and knowledge from those around them, so time with people focused on a goal can shape someone toward that goal. He illustrates the point with a year spent among people who make ice cream: a person would learn more about ice cream through exposure. He suggests spending time in communities aligned with one’s business model or goals. He explicitly says he is not telling viewers to cut everyone out. These are his claims and reasoning; the video does not establish that peer influence causes goal achievement.
