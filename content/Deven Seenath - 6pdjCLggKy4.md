## Summary

Speaker compares Shorts analytics and argues AVD, including replay-driven viewing above video length, matters more for reach than swipe ratio, though swipe still needs a claimed minimum. Examples: 24s AVD on 23s with 84.2% swipe; 32s on 27s with lower swipe; 14s on 7s with 83% swipe. He attributes revenue differences to RPM and geography: examples report 2 cents RPM with audience in India, Indonesia, Vietnam, Bangladesh, Philippines, versus 20 cents with audience in United States, India, Philippines, United Kingdom, Canada. He says broad testing on a new channel lowers swipe, mixed geography raises RPM. He claims views dip at 50% casual viewers, advises daily tracking, and recommends turning off “publish to subscription feed” for new viewers, escaping 30K view plateau, or niche transition. He says setting defaults on and changes only before upload/while private. He withholds narrow profitable niches due to lower TAM and copying risk, mentions view/comment bombing, then promotes mentorship and Discord testimonials. Claims unverified; captions only reviewed.
