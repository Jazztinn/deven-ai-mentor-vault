## Summary

Deven Seenath describes sponsorship rates by creator tier, follower count, and views: for creators with 10,000–30,000 followers and views, he gives $50–$75 per video, calling this “highballing,” and $5–$15 per video for faceless content. For 50,000–100,000 followers, he gives $100–$150 per video, with a possible $300 when product fit is good. For 500,000–1,000,000 followers, he says $1,000 per post is attainable with strong engagement and product fit. Separately, he reports receiving $1,500 for smaller accounts with only a few thousand views because of strong product fit. These are his estimates and anecdote, not verified or guaranteed income. He ends by inviting viewers to apply to his program for brand connections.
