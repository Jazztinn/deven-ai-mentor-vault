## Summary

Seenath outlines a YouTube automation workflow: choose a commentary-capable niche, source clips from social platforms, add commentary using a distinctive or personal voice, and edit in CapCut. He suggests scaling uploads from one per day to three and then five at two-hour intervals after stated view thresholds. He also recommends related videos to raise views per viewer, monitoring a channel trust score, market fit, competitors, and idea quality, and warns that falling channel and competitor views may signal a declining niche. He promotes a free 10-hour course and a bio link. Performance, voice-detection, and algorithm claims are source claims, not independently verified.
