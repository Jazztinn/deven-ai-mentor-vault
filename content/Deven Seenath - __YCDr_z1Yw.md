## Summary

Creator introduces YouTube Studio audience analytics, interpreting monthly audience trends and new/casual/regular viewer shares as indicators of channel growth or decline. He links these trends to views and revenue, proposes broader content when new-viewer growth reaches a claimed market ceiling, then gives upload-frequency targets tied to 48-hour views. He reports tests of higher daily posting volume and recommends related-video links to raise views per viewer. Earnings, algorithmic effects, and thresholds are his claims; captions contain inconsistent '48-hour'/'40 hour' wording. Creator recommends checking which audiences watch a channel, interpreting similar niche audiences as focused positioning and random audiences as either broad reach or mismatch. He says audience timing matters for long-form but not Shorts, calls out device type and increased TV viewing, and recommends exporting in 60 FPS and 4K to reach TV viewers. He also discusses audience formats and geography's effect on RPM, gives a US sports-channel RPM example and a Q4 prediction, then promotes a free course, coaching, Discord, and student results. Claims and forecasts remain attributed; captions do not identify the CapCut setting or support the causal claims.
