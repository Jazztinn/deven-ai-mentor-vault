## Summary

The speaker argues that long-form creators can add Shorts without abandoning long-form: use the “Formats your viewers watch” analytic as an audience signal, adapt the niche to proven short-form formats (including the hypothetical knitting-sock ranking examples), compare the channel’s long-form outliers with Shorts formats that already work, then connect broad Shorts through related videos toward long-form. He recommends sustained testing and says pickup can take longer than his proposed 8–12-day period. His rationale links repeat Shorts exposure to casual and regular viewers, a larger monthly audience, and more stable revenue; his definitions, trust-score explanations, and platform claims are the speaker’s account. He cites a student case with reported daily revenue rising from $500–$800 to $1,000–$4,000 and above $1,000 for almost three months, and also makes claims about $200,000 in additional or Shorts revenue. The ending promotes his mentorship and boot camp with claims about student outcomes.
