## Summary

Deven Seenath argues that Shorts can remain near a 30K-view plateau despite strong retention, and recommends examining audience mix, traffic sources, repeat viewing, subscription status, device type, and geography. He suggests consistent uploads, related-video links and calls to action, and testing new formats when new-viewer share is low. He also claims that turning off subscription-feed publishing can shift distribution. His explanations of monthly audience and YouTube recommendation behavior are unverified and sometimes unclear; for example, the monthly-audience passage claims at least 30 million views for a 30 million audience with consistent uploads. He gives device and geography RPM advice, including changing made-for-kids designation; that designation advice should not be followed when inaccurate. The close promotes one-on-one coaching using unverified student earnings claims of $10,000 to $60,000 per month, with some at $100,000. Findings retain platform and earnings statements as speaker claims. Captions only; visuals and audio not reviewed.
