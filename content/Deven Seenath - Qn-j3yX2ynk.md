## Summary

Speaker argues products that seem saturated can still reach other audiences through different marketing angles. His examples include framing an RC truck for women as a cute gift-delivery device, discussing skincare content aimed at men, and proposing a couples use for a charger. He recommends brainstorming people and situations in one’s life, asking why they might use or buy a product, and describing a reason or use rather than only its features. He says audience discovery takes experience and action. He reports sales and audience statistics, but captions do not verify them; his market, gender, and historical claims remain attributed and unverified. He also promotes a free Discord, calls, and one-on-one mentorship.
