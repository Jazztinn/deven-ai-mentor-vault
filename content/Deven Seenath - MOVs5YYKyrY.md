## Summary

Seenath says goals should identify a destination and actionable steps. He reports 84 million views in seven days and “around 5Kish” generated, with revenue blurred; original target timeframe appears as “100 million views a month or a week,” and he says he is behind. He urges hunger, harder weekly work, and consistent uploads, citing four uploads, three missed days, and management of 35 channels; he speculates more uploads might have met his goal. For a first 10,000-view goal, he describes studying similar videos, observing competition, and identifying improvements. He recommends a very high long-term target alongside baby steps, citing a trillion-view career goal, about 25 billion so far, and his stated 2.5% progress. For income, he gives two broad routes: product sales to a small audience or view-driven revenue from a large audience; he lists several businesses, then describes his product path as views, product creation, sales. He says YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok need platform-specific optimization and one platform’s virality may not transfer when targeting another platform’s audience. He reports 60K a month toward a $100,000 monthly goal; says 0-to-10K took about four years and 10-to-20 took two months, attributing easier later growth to learned skills and repeatability. Homework: journal goals and desired life, break goals into steps, and comment goals for his proposed feedback. Claims are attributed and unverified.
