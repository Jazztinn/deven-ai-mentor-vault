## Summary

Deven Seenath proposes three ways to scale social media: amplify emotion through visual or audible storytelling or useful information to hold attention; give viewers a reason to return, described through his “VPV” (“view per viewer”) framing; and upload consistently, which he claims increases an algorithmic trust score. He illustrates the advice with an attributed claim that a channel rose from $6,000 to $21,000 per month in a couple of weeks and says students scale similarly. He closes by inviting viewers to apply through the description. These are the creator’s recommendations and promotional claims; the metric framing, algorithm mechanism, revenue, student outcomes, and causal links are not verified.
