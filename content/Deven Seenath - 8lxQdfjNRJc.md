In this day-one business-progress vlog, Deven Seenath says he is documenting his business while working a 16-hour shift. He wants daily progress videos to track his development and let viewers compare later progress with the starting point, but feels business content clashes with the humor on his main channels and with the audiences of family, friends, and Instagram followers, so he plans a second account. He reports a clothing brand at roughly nine sales, about $60 per sale and $300 revenue after discounts, with 24 sales needed to break even; he regrets choosing clothing over dropshipping. He wants online business because he finds it fun and is passionate about it, while acknowledging high risk. He sees daily videos as a way to think clearly and hold himself accountable, says his work ethic and early-morning running have improved, and ends by deciding to focus on dropshipping. He also promotes a hoodie through Instagram. Captions contain garbled and conflicting store-revenue figures, and several channel/name words are unclear.
