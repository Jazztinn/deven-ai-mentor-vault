## Summary

Creator argues falling views may mean poor videos or a declining niche, and recommends diagnosing cause first. He reports a channel earning $300 to $600 a day while uploading about five videos daily before a trend niche declined. He says faceless channels depend on outside subjects, while a personal brand can remain the niche. He recommends competitor comparison, an 8 to 12 day “TB testing period” that may take longer after a high-casual-viewer niche, and review of new viewers, casual viewers, and monthly audience direction. For execution, he suggests studying competitor outliers, adapting prior winning concepts, finding shared premises or subtopics, and trying adjacent topics. Examples include hotels and yachts sharing people in them, ranking videos using audience reactions, and a cooking channel moving beyond pizza. He attributes view growth to correlations between successful videos and repeatable features. Ending promotes coaching and claims some students reach 10 to $30,000 per month within their first couple of weeks.
