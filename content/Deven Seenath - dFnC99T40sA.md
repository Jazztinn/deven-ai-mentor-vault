## Summary

Deven Seenath gives a sequence for addressing a claimed “30K view jail”: audit idea quality and competitor outlier videos; check swipe ratio and average view duration (AVD), improve weak metrics, then publish consistently for 8–12 days. He next recommends using related videos to increase views per viewer (VPV) and tracking monthly audience. If a channel remains stalled, he suggests turning off the publish-to-subscription-feed setting and waiting; if good metrics and a growing monthly audience still do not break the plateau after over a month, he suggests starting a new channel. He also points viewers to a free, approximately 40-hour YouTube course. Thresholds, timing, settings effects, and algorithm explanations are creator advice and remain unverified, not guarantees.
