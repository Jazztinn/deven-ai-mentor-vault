## Principles

### Automate a documented process and keep an owner for exceptions

Sources describe people or AI performing sourcing, writing, voice and editing, while other clips promise almost no owner work. A more qualified account explicitly says specific problems still require attention. Automation therefore describes task allocation in this corpus; it does not establish a passive-income guarantee.

**When It Applies:** A process needs a known standard, responsible person and capacity for mistakes or missing inputs.

**Researcher Application:** Map each step, owner, quality check and exception before delegating more work. Measure actual time saved.

**Limits:** Reported channel counts, workload reductions and revenue are unverified; a small week of owner work does not establish readiness for full handoff.

### Keep operating knowledge retrievable and correctable

Deven describes consolidating SOPs, reviews, calls, questions and transcripts into an AI reference. He also describes correcting a wrong answer and adding the correction to the shared database. Claims that it answers as well as him or replaces operator questions remain self-report.

**When It Applies:** Use when answers depend on accumulated operating knowledge; access and sensitive information require a defined boundary.

**Researcher Application:** Retain source passages, separate source claims from user observations, version corrections with their reason and evidence, and check a correction against the original before applying it broadly.

**Limits:** The source does not establish the model architecture, actual training method, evaluation results or public release. Versioning and provenance are researcher applications.

### Judge a production tool by the draft it enables

Source-era editing choices include CapCut, Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve; voice guidance includes expressive narration, inflection and language fit. AI tool rankings and future-capability predictions are time sensitive. A tool is a means of production, not evidence of likely reach.

**When It Applies:** Needs the user’s actual task, budget, hardware, current tool features and audio/visual sample.

**Researcher Application:** Use an available tool for one concrete draft. Listen and inspect the output before changing the tool or buying a service.

**Limits:** The cited prices, versions, releases, voice settings and performance predictions are not current verified recommendations.
