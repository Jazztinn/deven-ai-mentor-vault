## Summary

Across both chunks, speaker describes growing Instagram accounts by reposting popular TikTok material and connecting audience to Shopify products. He reports one account reached nearly 66,000 followers in one to two months and 15 million views, but says reused content did not produce large monthly income; one product yielded about $2,000 sales and $300–$400 profit, with weak sales. He recommends scouting TikTok niches, describes YouTube reposting as more competitive, and makes unverified claims about platform duplicate-content detection and trademark status. He proposes Instagram/Shopify dropshipping and YouTube AdSense/RPM, with music deal potentially adding $0.10 per thousand views, then argues for high-quality original videos and trend creation. Performance anecdotes include an unnamed channel at about 190,000 subscribers after roughly three weeks, $60 shortly after monetization, and reported daily views ranging from about one to five million, alongside claimed daily revenue estimates of $300–$600. In the ending, he claims YouTube view processing takes five days and says his advice is free and he has no course for sale. Legal, platform, timing, view, and earnings claims are attributed to speaker and remain unverified.
