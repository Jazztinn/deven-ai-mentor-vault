## Summary

The speaker proposes a YouTube short-form posting framework tied to the 48-hour view mark: begin at one upload daily, move to three around 50,000, then five around 140 or 150,000. He qualifies higher frequency as channel-dependent and recommends testing against view dips; examples include a channel that gained up to three daily posts but dipped at five, and another with no increase from five to nine. He also describes a three-channel collaboration approach and recommends 45 minutes between uploads plus related videos. Opening examples cite 70 million views and student earnings/growth; closing promotes a bootcamp and mentorship. All platform, growth, and earnings claims are attributed and unverified.
