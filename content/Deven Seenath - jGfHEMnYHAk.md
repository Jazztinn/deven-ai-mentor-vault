## Summary

The creator describes a business model for scaling automated YouTube Shorts channels through operators and editors. He recommends finding and training B-player editors, using Trello and virtual assistants to manage uploads, sharing revenue or equity with operators, and scaling one successful niche before entering another. His later advice includes upload-rate thresholds, related-channel saturation signals, a 60/40 balance of proven and experimental videos, and “replicate and reiterate” to adapt formats. The video includes promotional mentorship claims and multiple creator-reported revenue and growth examples. These claims and platform explanations are unverified.
